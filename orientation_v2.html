04 · Working Rhythm

How the work actually flows

01
Task assignment — from lead dev
Tasks come via GitHub Issues (or as agreed). Each is specific and scoped. If the brief is unclear — ask before starting, not after 2 hours of guessing.
02
Daily check-in with the mentor (~1h)
The mentor's daily hour is your primary support line. Bring blockers, progress updates, questions. Mentor reviews work before it reaches the lead dev.
03
Research & learning time (self-directed)
Some days are lighter — reading docs, experimenting locally. Always produce a tangible output: notes, a summary, or a short demo. No invisible work.
04
Weekly presentation (intern → whole team)
Every Friday: 5–10 min walkthrough of what was done, what was learned, what's next. No formal slides needed — just talk through it clearly.
05
Communication — async on [tool TBC]
All async messages go through the agreed tool. Respond within a reasonable window during working hours. No disappearing for more than a few hours.
Hours are flexible (2–5h/day) — but consistency and communication are non-negotiable
